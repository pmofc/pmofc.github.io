How to Earn From Crypto Content Without Holding Crypto (2026)

The assumption: you must hold crypto to write about it. The second: only traders can monetize from it. Both are wrong. Crypto content is a topic like any other — the people paying advertisers and affiliates for explanation, tax, security and risk content are the ones who don't hold (or only hold to understand). Here are the niches that pay and how to enter without a crypto balance.

In this guide

  1. The niches that pay
  2. Formats that convert
  3. Monetization paths

The niches that pay

  • Tax & accounting: how to report gains, cost basis — massive intent, finance advertisers love it.
  • Security: what to do when a wallet is compromised, hardware wallets, seed phrases — evergreen and critical.
  • Risk & education: "what is X really," stablecoin risks, DeFi failures — high sponsor fit.
  • Data narratives: charts of adoption by country, TVL growth — content + visuals.
  • Decision support: how to think about entering the space (for writers and researchers).

Formats that convert

  • Deep explainers (7–12 min, long-form) — best RPM for finance/crypto advertisers.
  • News commentary — timely, high CPC for exchanges/brands.
  • Beginner onboarding — addresses the "I don't know what I'm looking at" audience; great affiliate content for exchanges (see affiliate guide).
  • Security checklists & templates — high shareability, strong affiliate/ad fit.

Monetization paths

Crypto advertisers skew finance — CPMs of $20–60 per 1,000 impressions vs $2–10 general. That means crypto content often earns two to five times the general-ecosystem CPM while feeding the general ladder. Combine: AdSense for scale + exchange/trust affiliate programs for niche-depth. Full math in the traffic math post.

💡 The content angle: you don't need to believe in crypto — you need to understand it. "I don't hold" is honestly the most authentic and defensible creator stance, and it unlocks audiences that won't touch a tradfi channel.

Crypto content is a content vertical, not a portfolio requirement. If you can write clearly about money, security, or markets, you can monetize this niche — hold the coins or not.
