AdSense vs Ezoic vs Media.net: The Best Ad Network for Beginners

AdSense is where everyone starts — but it's rarely where blogs finish. Here's how the three beginner-reachable networks actually compare, and the upgrade path that turns your first approval into double the RPMs within a year.

In this guide

  1. The head-to-head table
  2. AdSense — the default (and why it's still right first)
  3. Ezoic — the first upgrade
  4. Media.net — the alternative
  5. The premium tier (for later)
  6. The exact path, month by month

1. The head-to-head table

2. AdSense — the default, and still right first

  • Pros: easiest to understand, tight Google policy alignment (great training wheels), instant credibility, no exclusivity.
  • Cons: lowest RPMs of the three; you manually manage every placement.
  • Use it when: you're pre-approval or under ~10k sessions/month. The approval process itself (see the checklist) disciplines your site into a better business.

3. Ezoic — the first upgrade

  • Pros: no traffic minimum; AI automatically tests thousands of layout/placement combinations — most sites see RPM lift 50%+ once testing converges; access to more advertisers than AdSense alone.
  • Cons: initial setup and learning curve; layout churn while tests run; rev-share scales with traffic so the cut grows as you do.
  • Use it when: you cross roughly 10k sessions/month and want RPM growth without becoming an ad-ops expert.

4. Media.net — the alternative

  • Pros: Yahoo/Bing advertiser demand (different pool from Google); strong on intent-heavy US/UK traffic; can run alongside or instead of AdSense.
  • Cons: approval is pickier; earnings swing more by niche and geography; contextual ad look isn't for every design.
  • Use it when: AdSense rejected you, or as a second network on high-intent pages (some publishers run AdSense + Media.net on different placements).

5. The premium tier (for later)

Know the destination even if it's far off: Monumetric (~10k+ pageviews), Mediavine (~50k sessions/month) and Raptive (100k+) pay dramatically higher RPMs because they sell your inventory directly to premium advertisers. Every upgrade decision below exists to reach these faster.

6. The exact path, month by month

  1. Months 1–4: build 20–30 quality posts → apply to AdSense (checklist). Keep affiliate income running in parallel (guide).
  2. Months 4–9: optimize placements manually; learn your RPM pattern (RPM math).
  3. ~10k sessions: trial Ezoic for 60–90 days; compare real RPM, not vibes. Keep whichever network wins.
  4. ~50k sessions: apply to Mediavine-class networks; that jump usually doubles everything again.
🧭 The one-line rule: AdSense to learn, Ezoic to optimize, premium networks to earn. Switching early wastes the education each tier gives you.
