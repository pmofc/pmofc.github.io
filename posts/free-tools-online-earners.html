25 Free Tools Every Online Earner Should Know

Software subscriptions are the silent tax on new online earners. This list is the workaround: free tiers and free-forever tools that cover 95% of what paid software does when you're starting out. Upgrade later, only when a tool is visibly making you money.

Writing & editing (5)

  • Google Docs — free word processor; share drafts with clients without emailing files.
  • Grammarly (free) — catches typos and clumsy sentences as you type anywhere in the browser.
  • Hemingway Editor — highlights overly long sentences; best free readability check there is.
  • QuillBot (free) — paraphraser and grammar check with daily free limits.
  • Notion (free) — drafts, content calendar, client notes in one place.

Design & graphics (5)

  • Canva Free — thumbnails, social posts, resumes, ebook covers. The single most useful free design tool.
  • Figma (free) — UI design and quick mockups; free personal plan.
  • Photopea — Photoshop in the browser, free, opens PSD files.
  • remove.bg — one-click transparent backgrounds for product photos.
  • Unsplash / Pexels — free high-quality stock photos with generous licenses.

Video & audio (4)

  • CapCut — free video editor with auto-captions; the standard for Shorts/TikTok.
  • DaVinci Resolve (free) — professional desktop video editing, genuinely free.
  • Audacity — free audio recording/editing for podcasts and voiceover.
  • OBS Studio — free screen recording and streaming; great for tutorial gigs.

SEO & research (5)

  • Google Search Console — the most valuable free SEO tool in existence; shows exactly which queries bring (or could bring) traffic.
  • Google Keyword Planner — free with a Google Ads account (don't need to run ads).
  • AnswerThePublic (free tier) — visualizes the questions people ask around a keyword.
  • Google Trends — compares topic interest over time and by region.
  • Bing Webmaster Tools — free site indexing + keyword data most people ignore.

Websites & hosting (3)

  • GitHub Pages — free static site hosting with free SSL; this blog's approach.
  • Netlify / Vercel (free tiers) — drag-and-drop deploys, free subdomains, generous free bandwidth.
  • Cloudflare Pages — free hosting with a world-class CDN behind it.

Money & productivity (3)

  • Gumroad / Payhip (free plans) — sell digital products; they take a per-sale cut instead of a subscription.
  • Wave — free invoicing and accounting for freelancers.
  • Toggl Track — free time tracking; essential for knowing your real hourly rate.
💡 The rule: a tool earns its upgrade only when it's measurably earning or saving you more than it costs. Until then, free tiers win — and this blog was built entirely with the tools above.
