How Much Traffic Do You Need to Earn $100/Month From AdSense?

One division problem answers this: needed sessions = target ÷ RPM × 1,000. The catch is that RPM varies 20× by niche — so the honest answer is a small table, not a number. Here it is.

In this guide

  1. The traffic table (by RPM)
  2. What this means by niche
  3. How to get there with LESS traffic
  4. Reality checks people ignore

1. The traffic table (by RPM)

Your RPM Monthly sessions for $100 Typical niche
$2 50,000 Entertainment, memes
$5 20,000 Food, lifestyle, general blogs
$10 10,000 Tech, education, travel
$20 5,000 Software reviews, B2B
$30 3,333 Finance, insurance, legal

2. What this means by niche

A food blogger needs 15× more traffic than a finance writer for the same $100. That's not a talent difference — it's advertiser demand. If $100/month is the goal, the single highest-leverage decision remains the niche: pick one with commercial intent and the traffic table tilts in your favor from day one.

3. How to get there with LESS traffic

  1. Stack affiliate income. A single $30 recurring SaaS commission ≈ what 2,500 sessions would earn in a low-RPM niche. Two or three of those and your “traffic requirement” halves. Guide: affiliate marketing with $0.
  2. Publish commercial-intent posts. “Best X for Y” articles earn 2–5× the RPM of informational ones and attract the affiliate clicks too.
  3. Improve ad placement. In-content units near the answer (not buried in sidebars) measurably raise RPM. This template's slots are placed that way for a reason.
  4. Attract high-value geographies. US/UK/CA/AU visitors pay multiples of low-bid markets. English content + search traffic helps naturally.
  5. Upgrade networks at ~10k sessions — Ezoic-style networks often beat raw AdSense RPMs. Comparison: AdSense vs Ezoic vs Media.net.

4. Reality checks people ignore

  • Traffic isn't linear in month one. New blogs crawl for months, then articles cluster onto page one together. The 20k milestone arrives in bursts, not a slope.
  • Sessions ≠ pageviews. AdSense RPM is measured per session; deeper engaged readers (2–3 pages per visit) earn more per session than bouncers.
  • Seasonality swings ±30%. Q4 highs, January lows — annualize, don't panic monthly.
  • $100/month isn't a wall, it's a checkpoint. The same machine that produces $100 produces $300; it just needs more posts pointing at commercial keywords.
🎯 Rule of thumb to remember: at a decent $10–15 RPM, plan on roughly 8,000–10,000 monthly sessions for $100/month from ads alone — or roughly half that traffic if you've layered affiliates into the same articles.

So the honest answer to “how much traffic for $100/month”: fewer than you think if the niche is right, more than you hoped if it isn't. Choose the niche first — then the traffic table works for you instead of against you.
