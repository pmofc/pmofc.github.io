Real RPM Payouts: AdSense vs Mediavine vs Raptive in 2026

You have a blog. It earns $4/month from AdSense. Somewhere a newsletter told you that "premium" networks pay more. This post puts real numbers behind that claim — what RPM actually looks like at each tier, and what would have to be true for you to jump.

In this guide

  1. The network ladder with real payouts
  2. Why premium beats AdSense
  3. The hidden costs of upgrading
  4. When (and how) to make the jump

The network ladder, with real payouts

Network Typical RPM Audience bar Payout threshold
AdSense $2 – $15 $0 – $20 CPC worlds Instant
Mediavine $10 – $30 ~10k monthly sessions 50k monthly pageviews
Raptive $15 – $50+ ~50k monthly sessions 100k monthly pageviews
📊 Reading the ladder: every tier roughly triples (sometimes quadruples) what you'd earn elsewhere. It's not "better technology" exclusively — it's buyer quality. You can reach AdSense's floor forever and never test; you can also climb for years and never climb enough to matter.

Why premium beats AdSense

It's mostly advertiser density, not platform magic. AdSense is Google buying display inventory; Mediavine and Raptive buy from premium direct-seller markets (premium video, finance, healthcare, SaaS). The same $1 RPM becomes $5+ when the advertiser is a bank and your post is about debt repayment. That's why niche choice matters as much as placement.

The hidden costs of upgrading

  • Traffic thresholds: you must serve a minimum number of sessions/pageviews before approval — your $200/month AdSense blog may be far below it.
  • Content requirements: premium networks favor established, content-rich sites; a brand-new site may be auto-rejected regardless of quality.
  • Revenue share: Mediavine takes ~30–40% initially; Raptive 20–30%. Still usually worth it.
  • Site speed/theme: premium networks want fast sites; make sure your free host + theme can deliver (this very template is built for that).

When (and how) to make the jump

  1. At ~10k monthly sessions (Mediavine zone), trial the upgrade; watch 60–90 days of real RPM before committing.
  2. At ~50k sessions, apply to Raptive — expect a content review; this is where the approval checklist pays off.
  3. Stack before merging: AdSense on one network + a premium on another via placement splits.
💸 Jump when the upside exceeds the friction: roughly, when your AdSense income (currently ~$4/mo at this blog's level) has crossed the premium network's threshold economics — i.e. when the gap between your RPM and the premium tier is worth the review risk. Most sites that reach the threshold do; most that don't stay on AdSense — correctly.
